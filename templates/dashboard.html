Model Interpretation

How the system evaluates suspicious URLs.

The system combines machine-learning predictions with URL-based security heuristics. URL length, domain structure, HTTPS usage, digits, special characters, subdomains and obfuscation-related features are evaluated alongside suspicious keywords, IP-based URLs and potential brand impersonation indicators.
